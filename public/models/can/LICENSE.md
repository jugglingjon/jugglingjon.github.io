Changes: the textures are downsized and re-encoded for the web (colour and
normal maps 1024 -> 512 px JPEG, metal/roughness 1024 -> 256 px PNG) with
scripts/slim-glb.py; the geometry is unchanged. The site scales it to size at
runtime.
