# Digital Radio Clock

"Digital Radio Clock" (https://sketchfab.com/3d-models/digital-radio-clock-23b2d0e81d414836877c555e5b5fc18d)
by NoMoreFeelings (https://sketchfab.com/NoMoreFeelings), licensed under
Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International (CC BY 4.0):
http://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/

Changes: the texture is downsized and re-encoded for the web (1024 px PNG ->
512 px JPEG), and an emissive map is added so the clock's LED digits and the
tuning dial glow, both with scripts/slim-glb.py; the geometry is unchanged.
The site scales it to size and makes its material matte at runtime.
